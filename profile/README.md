## What Is Montax Imposer

Montax Imposer is a sheet-layout helper for people who print booklets, cards, and short runs themselves. Montax Imposer booklet layout is the folded path: pages are paired so the sheet can be stacked and folded in half. Montax Imposer n-up sheet is the flat path: several copies sit side by side for cards or class handouts. A student printing a zine, or a small office printing a program, can stay with one of those two ideas.

Montax Imposer signature split matters when the booklet is too thick to fold as one stack. Montax Imposer crop marks tell the trimmer, or a careful pair of scissors, where the page edge belongs. Montax Imposer creep shift answers the shove that appears when many sheets are nested: inner pages would otherwise stick out and get cut too close. Those three checks keep a modest job from looking accidental.

The daily loop is short. Choose Montax Imposer booklet layout or Montax Imposer n-up sheet, add Montax Imposer crop marks, and turn on Montax Imposer creep shift only when the paper stack is thick. Use Montax Imposer signature split when the piece is long enough to need sections. Then print a single proof before the whole run.
